    From antenna to image

    How SagapSat handles a pass over the Metro Manila station.

    1. Antenna

      Receives the satellite’s VHF broadcast.

    2. RTL-SDR

      Samples the received signal as I/Q.

    3. Raspberry Pi and SatDump

      Records the pass and decodes image frames. Selected recordings can be reprocessed on the Mac.

    4. SagapSat archive

      Publishes image products, decoded frames, and available reception logs.

    COMPARE · GPM / IMERG

    96 source files verified

    All 96 original IMERG Late Run files are preserved and hash-checked. Offline replay verifies 144 box/hour records and 2,736 base/shift records. The base sampling uses one western and two eastern native cells, limiting fine-scale interpretation.

    Area-weighted rainfall estimates

    We now weight each native rainfall cell by its spherical overlap with the fixed study box. None of the four original September wet-hour contrasts is east-greater in all three area-weighted products. At 16:00 UTC on 17 September, IMERG changes from +0.585 mm with cell centers to −0.170 mm with area overlaps. Expanded 0.30° boxes provide a separate sensitivity check.

    October follow-up

    Fixed October 6, 16 and 26 checks include all 72 cloud snapshots and 68 complete two-product rain-hour comparisons. All 37 easterly hours have rain data, but none meets the qualifying east-greater wet criterion. Four October 26 CMORPH source containers are empty and remain missing, never dry.

    A separately declared October screen retains all 124 candidates at 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC. Three wet/easterly hours qualify on October 11–12: two have opposing product signs. The concordant October 12 case has 98.2% western and 100% eastern cloud occupancy, so complete saturation is not universal. These adjacent-date cases do not establish independent-storm replication; IMERG was not retrieved for them.

    Local projection checks have not established independent landmark accuracy. No suitable in-box gauge record was obtained from the audited public station sources. Local quantitative geography, ground validation, author/adviser review and organizer clarification remain open.

      Evidence & method

      Receive & preserve

      Original CADUs and processing logs remain intact. This portal copies selected evidence without changing the source archive. Raw IQ is retained separately on the Pi where documented.

      Check the frames

      Count complete 1024-byte records, distinct records, expected sync markers, and trailing bytes. SHA-256 hashes connect each download to its archived source.

      Keep the limits visible

      Decoded products can contain missing lines. Colour composites are variants of the same observation. Geographic overlays are withheld pending scan-time validation.
